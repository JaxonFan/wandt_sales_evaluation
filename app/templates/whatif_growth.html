What-if · cumulative profit-growth preview — pays nothing

A read-only sandbox for the proposed replacement of the Growth piece. Nothing here is paid or recorded — it shows how the model would score the cycle on live data, beside today's number. The rule: each account's cumulative profit this cycle is compared to what that same account did a year ago, month by month; you're paid {{ '%.1f'|format(rate*100) }}% of the increment, split among the reps working it by work-share. It's a progressive true-up — each month pays only the new ground above the prior peak, so a down month just pauses pay (never clawed back). A flat account pays ~$0; a brand-new account has no year-ago, so its whole profit counts as the increment.
