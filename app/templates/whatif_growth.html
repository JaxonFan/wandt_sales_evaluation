What-if · cumulative profit-growth preview — pays nothing

A read-only sandbox for the proposed replacement of the Growth piece. Nothing here is paid or recorded — it just shows how the model would score the current cycle on live data, beside today's number. The rule: each account is measured against what that same account did a year ago (profit), and that growth is split among the reps working it by their share of the account (work-share) — so inheriting a flat account pays ~$0; you earn by actually growing it. Cumulative from an August start (month 1 = this Aug vs last Aug), trued-up each period; a down month just pauses pay (self-correcting, no clawback). New accounts (< 12 months old) earn 1% of their profit instead — the landing/acquisition bonus is separate.
