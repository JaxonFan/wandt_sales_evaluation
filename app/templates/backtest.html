How to read this — we score the account, not who sold it before

Each rep is measured on the accounts they work now. For every account we compare its profit this year to what that same account made a year ago — it doesn't matter who sold it back then. Whoever works it now is simply measured against the account's own past. A real example:

The accountNEW BEN CITY 檳城 made $4,119 profit last January.
Who works itLast year An Cao sold it; this year Vanessa does.
How we score itVanessa is measured against what NEW BEN CITY did last year ($4,119). She earns only if she grows it past that — she gets no free credit for taking it over, and An Cao is not charged for an account he no longer works.

Because we always compare an account to its own past, it's fair when accounts move between reps — or when someone leaves (e.g. Maple Tran): whoever picks up the account is never handed free growth, and no one is blamed for losing an account.
