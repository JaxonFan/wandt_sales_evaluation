1开单奖 — 每行 ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}

酬劳的是日常铺货的功夫，和金额无关。

  • 一张发票上有几行商品，就算几行。一行 ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}。
  • 只算销售自己写的发票（批次号前两位决定是谁）。
  • 作废发票不算。
例子9 月 An Cao 写了 1,256 行 → 1,256 × ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} = $125.60。在「我的奖金」和 Dashboard 的 Contribution 一栏都能看到这个数和行数。

2增长奖 — 按小组算

客户很难算在某一个人头上，所以增长奖按小组：把小组名下每一个客户的利润，和同一个客户去年同期比，全部加在一起。

小组

    {% for t, ms in teams.items() %}
  • {{ t }}：{{ ms|join('、') }}
    • {% endfor %}
  • House：经理，以及 house 客户（First Choice、Enson、散客 CASH & CARRY）—— 不参与增长奖。

客户归谁

  • 最近 {{ window_months }} 个月里，哪个小组写了这个客户 {{ '%.0f'|format(pct) }}% 以上的订单（按单数，不看金额），客户就归哪个组。
  • 没有小组过线的是共有客户，在 Accounts 页把它拖到一个小组 —— 没指定之前谁都拿不到它的增长奖。你的指定优先于自动判断（显示「pinned」）。

怎么算

小组净增长 = Σ 每个客户 (今年至今利润 − 去年同期利润)
目标 = 目标% × 小组这些客户去年利润（默认 {{ '%.0f'|format(default_target*100) }}%，Settings 按组调）
增长奖 = {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% × min(净增长, 目标) + {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}% × max(0, 净增长 − 目标)
// 每月只对「超过之前高点」的部分发；跌的月份 $0，永不倒扣；小组奖金平分给组员
  • 算客户，不算个人。客户换人时历史跟着客户走：接手平稳客户不算增长，被调走也不扣分。
  • 整本账一起算。一个客户涨不能掩盖另一个客户跌，看净和。
  • 周期每年十月重置：十月比去年十月，十月+十一月比去年十月+十一月，一直到九月。
例子檳城超級市場 2025 年 1 月做了 $4,119 利润（当时 An Cao 负责）。2026 年归 Team 2。Team 2 只有做到超过 $4,119 才算增长 —— 接手不白给，An Cao 也不因此扣分。

4按收款发放 — 按月算

以上算出来的都是已赚。实际可发看客户有没有把货款付清，而且每个月单独算。

本月已释放 = 本月已赚 × (本月发票已收款 ÷ 本月发票总额)
本月欠付 = 已释放 − 已对本月发放
现在可发 = 本月欠付 + 以前月份还没发的
  • 八月的钱只看八月的发票，九月只看九月 —— 不混在一起算。
  • 发放时先结清最早的月份。
  • 没发的钱永远不会过期。客户十一月才付九月的货款，九月的奖金就十一月发，就算已经进入下一个周期。
  • 作废、坏账发票不算；已收款后又退票，这张发票会自动退出。
例子Ting Ting 8 月赚 $99.40，8 月发票收回 42% → 释放 $41.72；9 月赚 $91.20，收回 33% → 释放 $29.79。现在可发 $71.51。等 8 月剩下的发票收回来，8 月那部分再继续释放。

6每月流程

  1. 从 GP 导出三个文件，在 Import 逐个导入：销售发票、已收款、作废发票。
  2. 打开 Accounts，把「Needs assignment」里的共有客户拖到小组（不指定就没人拿增长奖）。
  3. 看 New accounts，标注 rep-won / house。
  4. 看 Falling behind 和 Quiet accounts，安排跟进。
  5. 回到 Dashboard，核对每个人的 Pay now（点开行看发票），然后点 Pay 记录发放。

7特殊情况

  • 限购商品（Limited stock）：两年都剔除 —— 缺货不算销售的，去年卖过的也不抬高基数。
  • 关店：默认计入（真实损失）；确认无过失可豁免，同样两年都剔除。
  • 一次性大单：当年照发，第二年不重复也没关系，奖金永远不会低于 $0。
  • 离职员工（如 Maple Tran）的客户：历史保留，接手的人只有做过去年才有增长。
  • 退票：已收款文件里不再出现的发票会自动退出，对应奖金不再释放。

8常见问题

为什么 An Cao 收款比例比 Wendy 低很多？

看客户，不看人。大连锁付款周期长，小店付得快。钱不会少，只是晚点释放。

换月份的时候，为什么以前的月份没有 Pay 按钮？

过去的月份只读。它没发完的钱已经算进最新月份的 Pay now 里，按钮下面会写「+N earlier」。

两个小组账本差不多大，但一个两人一个三人，公平吗？

用 Settings 里的小组目标%来平衡 —— 人多的组目标可以定低一点。

怎么换小组成员？

需要在周期边界（十月一日）做，因为会重算客户归属和平分比例。目前由开发者修改配置。

1Contribution — ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} per line

Rewards the everyday work of placing products, regardless of dollar size.

  • Each product line on an invoice counts once: ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} a line.
  • Only invoices the rep wrote (the batch-number prefix says who).
  • Voided invoices count for nothing.
ExampleIn September An Cao wrote 1,256 lines → 1,256 × ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} = $125.60. The Contribution column shows the amount with the line count in brackets; click the row to see the invoices.

2Growth — a team number

Accounts are hard to pin on one person, so growth is earned by the team: every account the team owns, each compared to that same account a year ago, added together.

The teams

    {% for t, ms in teams.items() %}
  • {{ t }}: {{ ms|join(', ') }}
    • {% endfor %}
  • House: managers plus the house accounts (First Choice, Enson, the walk-in CASH & CARRY) — earns no growth.

Who owns an account

  • The team that wrote {{ '%.0f'|format(pct) }}% or more of its orders over the last {{ window_months }} months — counted in orders, not dollars.
  • No team at the bar → the account is shared. Drag it onto a team on the Accounts page — until you do, it pays nobody. Your choice always beats the rule (shown as pinned).

The math

team net growth = Σ over its accounts (profit this cycle − same account, same months last year)
target = target% × the team's last-year profit on those accounts (default {{ '%.0f'|format(default_target*100) }}%, per team in Settings)
growth pay = {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% × min(net, target) + {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}% × max(0, net − target)
// pays only new ground above the previous peak; a down month is $0, never clawed back; split equally among members
  • Accounts, not people. An account carries its history when it changes hands: inheriting a flat account isn't growth, and losing one isn't a penalty.
  • Whole book, netted. One growing account can't pay out while the rest shrinks more.
  • The cycle resets each October: October vs last October, Oct+Nov vs last Oct+Nov, … through September.
ExampleNEW BEN CITY made $4,119 profit in January 2025 under An Cao. In 2026 it belongs to Team 2. Team 2 earns only if it grows the account past $4,119 — no free credit for taking it over, and An Cao is not charged for losing it.

4Pay on collection — month by month

Everything above is earned. What's actually payable follows the customers' payments, and each month stands on its own.

released_M = earned_M × (M's invoices collected ÷ M's invoices billed)
owed_M = released_M − paid against M
pay now = owed on this month + anything still unpaid from earlier months
  • August's earnings look only at August's invoices; September's only at September's — never blended.
  • A payment settles the oldest month first.
  • Unpaid money never expires. If a customer pays a September invoice in November, September's bonus releases in November — even though the cycle has rolled over.
  • Voided and written-off invoices count for nothing. A payment that bounces drops the invoice back out automatically.
ExampleTing Ting earned $99.40 in August; 42% of August's invoices are paid → $41.72 released. September: $91.20 earned, 33% paid → $29.79. Pay now $71.51. As the rest of August's invoices get paid, August keeps releasing.

6Monthly routine

  1. Export the three files from GP and drop each into Import: sales invoices, paid invoices, voided invoices.
  2. Open Accounts and drag every "Needs assignment" account onto a team — unassigned accounts pay nobody.
  3. Review New accounts: rep-won or house.
  4. Check Falling behind and Quiet accounts — who follows up.
  5. Back on the Dashboard, open each rep's row to check Pay now against the invoices, then press Pay to record it.

7Edge cases

  • Limited stock (tab): flagged items are removed from both years — a "couldn't ship it" decline isn't charged, and last year's sales of it don't raise the bar.
  • Store closures: count against the book by default (a real loss); a confirmed no-fault closure can be exempted, again from both years.
  • One-time big orders: paid that year; if it doesn't repeat, fine — a bonus can never go below $0.
  • Staff who left (e.g. Maple Tran): their accounts keep their history; whoever picks one up earns only above last year.
  • Reversed payments: an invoice that disappears from the paid file drops out, and its bonus stops releasing.

8FAQ

Why is An Cao's collected % so much lower than Wendy's?

It's the customers, not the rep. Big chains pay on long terms; independents pay fast. Nothing is lost — it releases later.

Why does a past month have no Pay button?

Past months are read-only. Whatever they still owe is already inside the latest month's Pay now — the chip under the button says "+N earlier".

The two teams have similar books but 2 vs 3 people — is the equal split fair?

Use the per-team target % in Settings to balance it; the bigger team can carry a lower target.

How do I move someone to another team?

Do it at a cycle boundary (October 1), because ownership and the split are recomputed. For now it's a configuration change made by the developer.
