一共三项，每项都是一条谁都能核对的直接公式。只按已收货款发放。

1 · 增长奖 — 净利润增长的 {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}%

一句话规则：把销售手上每一个客户的利润，和同一个客户去年同期比，全部加在一起 —— 总数为正，销售拿 {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}%；为负，$0。

  • 算客户，不算个人。客户换人时历史跟着客户走。例子：An Cao 在 2025 年 1 月做了檳城超級市場 $4,119 利润；2026 年这个客户转给了 Vanessa。它离开 An Cao 的账本时连同基数一起离开（不扣他的分）， Vanessa 接手时连同基数一起接手（只有做过 $4,119 才有增长）。调客户对双方都不加分、不减分 —— 离职员工（如 Maple Tran）的客户也一样保留历史。
  • 整本账一起算。一个客户涨不能掩盖其它客户跌 —— 客户1 + 客户2 + … 的净和才算数。
  • 共同负责的客户。两个销售一起做一个客户时，这个客户的同比变化按各自当前的业务占比拆分 —— 这样年中换人也是中性的（客户的历史不会被重复发放）。
  • 逐月累计 + 补差发放。每月比较累计数（今年至今 vs 去年同期至今），只对超过之前高点的 新增部分发放。跌的月份 $0，永不倒扣。季节性自然抵消。
  • 一次性大单：客户偶尔下一笔特别大的订单，当年就照发 —— 是他们赚来的。第二年这笔通常不会重复， 账面会显得低一些；没关系，销售只是不会为同一笔生意再拿一次钱。而且奖金永远不会低于 $0， 所以一次性大单只会帮到销售，绝不会让他们倒亏。
  • 限购商品（Limited stock）：两年都剔除 —— 缺货造成的下滑不算销售的， 去年卖过的也不抬高基数。
  • 关店：默认计入（真实的利润损失，销售是我们的预警系统）。确认无过失关店可以豁免 —— 同样是两年都剔除。
  • 周期每年八月重置（计划启动时间）。本页目前按 2026 年 1–6 月运行；八月正式启动后周期锚点改为八月。

2 · 开单奖 — 每行 ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}

发票每一行 ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}。奖励日常铺货的功夫，稳定、好核对。

3 · 新客户奖 — 开发新客户的一次性奖励

销售自己开发的全新客户（在New accounts页确认「rep-won」）， 约一个季度后按规模一次性发：

  • 小（年化 < ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_small) }}）→ ${{ flat_small }}
  • 中（年化 < ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_medium) }}）→ ${{ flat_medium }}
  • 大（年化 ≥ ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_medium) }}）→ ${{ flat_large }}

新客户没有去年基数，它的全部利润同时自动计入增长 —— 一次性奖励酬开发，增长奖酬经营。

怎么区分是销售开发的还是客户自己打电话来的？电话没法查证，所以我们不去猜。 默认按 house（不发）—— 需要销售主动申报一个真实的开发，由经理判断是否合理（是否在他的片区、 是否是他已经在做的连锁店的分店等）。影响很小：金额不大，而且不管算不算，客户的利润照样计入增长和开单。

按收款发放

以上都是目标数。实际可发 = 目标 × 该销售货款回收比例（来自应收上传）。款到多少、发多少， 差额随收款自动释放，不会多发。作废、坏账发票一律不算。

每月流程

  1. 从 GP 导出并导入：销售发票、已收款、作废发票。
  2. 看New accounts —— 标注 rep-won / house。
  3. 看Dashboard：每人净增长、当月发放、现在可发。

参数（比例、开单单价、新客户档位、限购清单）在主记分卡应用的 Settings 里管理 —— 两个应用共用同一个数据库。

Three pieces, each a direct formula anyone can check. Paid only on collected money.

1 · Growth — {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% of your book's net profit increase

The rule in one sentence: take every account a rep works, add up how much more (or less) profit each one made than that same account made in the same months last year — if the total is positive, the rep earns {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% of it; if it's negative, $0.

  • Accounts, not salespeople. An account carries its history when it changes hands. Example: An Cao sold NEW BEN CITY $4,119 in Jan 2025; in 2026 it moved to Vanessa. It leaves his book with its baseline (doesn't count against him) and she inherits it with the baseline (earns only above $4,119). Handoffs are neutral — including accounts from staff who left.
  • Whole book, netted. One growing account can't pay out while the rest of the book shrinks more — account 1 + account 2 + … must be net positive.
  • Shared accounts. When two reps work the same account, that account's YoY change is split by each rep's current share of it — so a mid-year handoff stays neutral (the account's history isn't double-paid).
  • Cumulative + true-up. Each month we compare the running totals (this year so far vs the same months last year) and pay only new ground above the previous peak. A down month pays $0 — and is never clawed back. Seasonality cancels out by construction.
  • One-time big orders. If a customer places one unusually large order, the rep is paid on it that year — they earned it. The next year that order usually won't repeat, so the account looks lower; that's fine — the rep just isn't paid again for a sale that only happened once. Because a rep's bonus can never drop below $0, a one-off can only ever help them, never cost them.
  • Limited stock (tab): flagged items are removed from both years — a "couldn't ship it" decline is not charged to the rep, and last year's sales of it don't raise the bar.
  • Store closures. By default a closure counts against the book (it's a real loss, and the rep is the early-warning system). For a confirmed no-fault closure you can exempt the account — removed from both years, same apples-to-apples idea.
  • The cycle resets each August (program launch). This page currently runs Jan–Jun 2026; at the August launch the cycle anchor moves to August.

2 · Contribution — ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} per line item

Every invoice line the rep writes earns ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}. Rewards the everyday work of placing products — steady, effort-based, easy to verify.

3 · Acquisition — a flat bonus for landing a new account

When a rep wins a brand-new account (you confirm rep-won vs house on the New accounts tab), they get a one-time flat bonus at the ~quarter mark, once the account's size is clear:

  • Small (< ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_small) }}/yr run-rate) → ${{ flat_small }}
  • Medium (< ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_medium) }}/yr) → ${{ flat_medium }}
  • Large (≥ ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_medium) }}/yr) → ${{ flat_large }}

The new account's profit also feeds the growth net automatically (it has no year-ago, so its whole profit is its gap) — the flat bonus rewards the landing, growth rewards the building.

How to tell rep-won from a call-in? There's no way to verify a phone call, so we don't try. The default is house (no bonus) — the rep must claim a genuine win and you decide if it's plausible (their territory, a sister store they already serve, etc.). It's low-stakes on purpose: the amount is small and the account earns growth + contribution regardless.

Pay on collection

Everything above is a target. What's actually payable scales with the share of the rep's billing that has been collected (from your paid-invoices upload). As money comes in, the remainder releases — a true-up that never overpays. Voided and written-off invoices count toward nothing.

Monthly routine

  1. Export from GP and import: sales invoices, paid invoices, voided invoices.
  2. Review the New accounts tab — mark rep-won vs house.
  3. Read the Dashboard: each rep's net gap, the month's pay, and Payable now.

Dials (rate, item rate, acquisition tiers, limited-stock list) are managed in the main scorecard app's Settings — both apps read the same database.
