Manager guide — the new bonus, in plain language

Three pieces, each a direct formula anyone can check. Paid only on collected money.

1 · Growth — {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% of your book's net profit increase

The rule in one sentence: take every account a rep works, add up how much more (or less) profit each one made than that same account made in the same months last year — if the total is positive, the rep earns {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% of it; if it's negative, $0.

  • Accounts, not salespeople. An account carries its history when it changes hands. Example: An Cao sold NEW BEN CITY $4,119 in Jan 2025; in 2026 it moved to Vanessa. It leaves his book with its baseline (doesn't count against him) and she inherits it with the baseline (earns only above $4,119). Handoffs are neutral — including accounts from staff who left.
  • Whole book, netted. One growing account can't pay out while the rest of the book shrinks more — account 1 + account 2 + … must be net positive. Shared accounts split by each rep's share of its profit.
  • Cumulative + true-up. Each month we compare the running totals (this year so far vs the same months last year) and pay only new ground above the previous peak. A down month pays $0 — and is never clawed back. Seasonality cancels out by construction; a slow month is made up by a strong one.
  • One-off orders. A one-time windfall pays when it happens and simply makes next year's compare harder by the same amount — a wash over two years, and the floor at $0 means the rep can never owe.
  • Limited stock. Items you flag as supply-constrained are removed from both years — a "couldn't ship it" decline is not charged to the rep, and last year's sales of it don't raise the bar.
  • Store closures. By default a closure counts against the book (it's a real loss, and the rep is the early-warning system). For a confirmed no-fault closure you can exempt the account — removed from both years, same apples-to-apples idea.
  • The cycle resets each August (program launch). This page currently backtests Jan–Jun 2026.

2 · Contribution — ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} per line item

Every invoice line the rep writes earns ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}. Rewards the everyday work of placing products — steady, effort-based, easy to verify.

3 · Acquisition — a flat bonus for landing a new account

When a rep wins a brand-new account (you confirm rep-won vs house on the New accounts tab), they get a one-time flat bonus at the ~quarter mark, once the account's size is clear:

  • Small (< ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_small) }}/yr run-rate) → ${{ flat_small }}
  • Medium (< ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_medium) }}/yr) → ${{ flat_medium }}
  • Large (≥ ${{ '{:,}'.format(tier_medium) }}/yr) → ${{ flat_large }}

The new account's profit also feeds the growth net automatically (it has no year-ago, so its whole profit is its gap) — the flat bonus rewards the landing, growth rewards the building.

Pay on collection

Everything above is a target. What's actually payable scales with the share of the rep's billing that has been collected (from your paid-invoices upload). As money comes in, the remainder releases — a true-up that never overpays. Voided and written-off invoices count toward nothing.

Monthly routine

  1. Export from GP and import: sales invoices, paid invoices, voided invoices.
  2. Review the New accounts tab — mark rep-won vs house.
  3. Read the Dashboard: each rep's net gap, the month's pay, and Payable now.

Dials (rate, item rate, acquisition tiers, limited-stock list) are managed in the main scorecard app's Settings — both apps read the same database. This preview pays nothing until you adopt it.
