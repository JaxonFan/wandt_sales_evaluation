1 · 增长奖 — 按小组算，到目标 {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}%，超过目标 {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}%

一句话：客户很难分清算谁的，所以增长奖按小组算：把小组名下所有客户的利润加在一起， 和同样这批客户去年同期比，多出来的部分 —— 到小组目标之前拿 {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}%， 超过目标的部分拿 {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}%；如果整体比去年少，这个月是 $0（不倒扣）。 小组的增长奖平分给每个组员。

  • 我们的小组：{% for t, ms in teams.items() %}{{ t }}（{{ ms|join('、') }}）{% if not loop.last %}；{% endif %}{% endfor %}。 {% if my_team %}你在 {{ my_team }}。{% endif %}
  • 客户归哪个组：最近 12 个月里，一个小组写了这个客户 80% 以上的订单（按单数算， 不看金额），客户就归这个组；没有小组过线的客户由经理指定。
  • 开单奖和新客户奖还是算个人的 —— 自己写的每一行、自己开发的新客户，都是自己的。
  • 算客户，不算个人。客户换人负责时，它的历史跟着客户走：接手一个平稳的客户不算增长， 也不会因为客户被调走而吃亏 —— 调整客户对谁都不加分、不减分。
  • 全部客户一起算。一个客户涨、另一个跌，看的是加总后的净增长。红色（下滑）的客户 值得先去拜访。
  • 逐月累计、只对新高发放。淡季旺季自然抵消；某个月跌了就是 $0，做回来、超过之前的高点， 又继续发。已经发的不会收回。
  • 一次性大单：偶尔的一笔大单，当年照发；第二年不重复也没关系 —— 你只是不会为同一笔生意 再拿一次钱，而且奖金永远不会低于 $0，绝不会倒亏。
  • 新客户没有去年可比，所以它的全部利润都算进我们的净增长。
  • 限购商品（公司缺货的）两年都剔除 —— 供不上货不算我们的问题。

我们自己开发的新客户（经理确认后），开单约一个季度、规模看清楚了，一次性发： 小客户 ${{ flat_small }} · 中客户 ${{ flat_medium }} · 大客户 ${{ flat_large }}。 之后这个客户的利润还会继续算进增长奖。

按收款发放

以上算出来的是目标数。实际发放按我们客户货款的回收比例 —— 收到多少货款、就发多少比例， 剩下的等款到了自动补上。作废、坏账的发票不算。

  • 打开「落后客户」看谁在掉 —— 下滑的客户早点回访，比什么都有效。
  • 帮客户把货款按时结清 —— 收款比例上去了,「现在可发」马上跟着涨。
  • 多开新品、多开新客户 —— 每一行都有开单奖，新客户还有一次性奖励。
  • 第一次登录后，请在「修改密码」里把密码改成自己的。

1 · Growth — a team number: {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% up to target, {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}% above

One sentence: accounts are hard to pin on one person, so growth is earned by your team: add up the profit of every account your team owns and compare it to what those same accounts did last year — on the increase the team earns {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% up to its target and {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}% above it; if the total is below last year, that month is $0 (never clawed back). The team's growth pay is split equally among its members.

  • The teams: {% for t, ms in teams.items() %}{{ t }} ({{ ms|join(', ') }}){% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}. {% if my_team %}You are on {{ my_team }}.{% endif %}
  • Which team owns an account: the team that wrote 80% or more of its orders over the last 12 months (counted in orders, not dollars). Accounts no team owns outright are assigned by the manager.
  • Contribution and new-account bonuses stay yours — the lines you write and the accounts you land are counted for you personally.
  • Accounts, not people. When an account changes hands its history travels with it — inheriting a flat account isn't growth, and losing an account doesn't hurt you. Reassignments are neutral.
  • Whole book together. One account up, another down — the net is what pays. The red (declining) ones are worth visiting first.
  • Cumulative, pays on new highs. Slow and strong months cancel out; a down month is $0, recover past your peak and it pays again. Nothing already paid is taken back.
  • One-time big orders: an occasional large order pays that year; if it doesn't repeat next year that's fine — you just don't get paid twice for the same sale, and you can never end up owing.
  • New accounts have no last year, so all their profit counts toward your net.
  • Limited-stock items are removed from both years — supply problems aren't your problem.

2 · Contribution — ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }} per line

Every line item you write on an invoice earns ${{ '%.2f'|format(item_rate) }}.

3 · Acquisition — a one-time bonus for landing a new account

A new account you won yourself (manager-confirmed) pays once its size is clear (~a quarter): small ${{ flat_small }} · medium ${{ flat_medium }} · large ${{ flat_large }}. Its profit keeps counting toward your growth too.

Paid on collection

Everything above is a target. What's actually paid follows your customers' payments — collect the money and the rest releases automatically. Voided and written-off invoices never count.

What you can do

  • Open "Falling behind" — an early visit to a slipping customer beats everything.
  • Help customers settle invoices on time — your collected % rises and "payable now" follows.
  • Place more lines, land new accounts — every line pays, and a new account pays extra.
  • After your first sign-in, set your own password under My account.
