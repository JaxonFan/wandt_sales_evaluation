客户归哪个组

  • 最近 12 个月里，哪个组写了这个客户 80% 以上的订单（按单数），客户就归哪个组。没有组过线的由经理指定。

小组净增长 = Σ 每个客户 (今年至今利润 − 去年同期利润)
增长奖 = {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% × 到目标的部分 + {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}% × 超过目标的部分
我的一份 = 小组增长奖 ÷ 组员人数

4什么时候发钱 — 按月、按收款

上面算出来的是已赚。实际发放看客户有没有把货款付清，而且每个月单独算。

本月已释放 = 本月已赚 × (本月发票客户已付 ÷ 本月发票总额)
现在可发 = 已释放 − 已发给我的（加上以前月份还没发的）
  • 八月的奖金只看八月的发票收没收回来；九月只看九月。
  • 没发的钱不会作废。客户十一月才付九月的货款，九月那部分奖金就十一月发，就算已经进入下一个周期。
  • 作废、坏账的发票不算。
例子8 月赚 $99.40，8 月发票客户付了 42% → 释放 $41.72；9 月赚 $91.20，付了 33% → 释放 $29.79。现在可发 $71.51。8 月剩下的发票付清后，8 月那部分继续发。

7常见问题

我的货款已收比例为什么比别人低？

看客户不看人：大连锁付款慢，小店付得快。钱不会少，只是晚点发。

跌的月份会倒扣吗？

不会。已经发的永远不收回；跌的月份增长奖是 $0，做回来再接着发。

客户被调给别人了，我吃亏吗？

不吃亏。历史跟着客户走，调走的客户不再算在我们组，也不扣我们的分。

组里有人卖得多、有人卖得少，为什么平分？

增长奖按小组算是因为客户很难分清是谁的；开单奖和新客户奖还是各算各的。

2Growth — a team number

Accounts are hard to pin on one person, so growth is earned by your team: every account the team owns, compared to what those same accounts earned last year.

Which team owns an account

  • The team that wrote 80% or more of its orders over the last 12 months (by count). Accounts no team owns outright are assigned by the manager.
  • Accounts, not people. When an account changes hands its history travels with it — inheriting a flat account isn't growth, and losing one doesn't cost you.

The math

team net growth = Σ over its accounts (profit this cycle − same account last year)
growth pay = {{ '%.0f'|format(rate*100) }}% × the part up to target + {{ '%.1f'|format(accel*100) }}% × the part above
your share = team growth pay ÷ number of members

4When you get paid — month by month, on collection

Everything above is earned. What's actually paid follows your customers' payments, and each month stands on its own.

released this month = earned this month × (this month's invoices paid ÷ this month's invoices billed)
payable now = released − already paid to you (plus anything still unpaid from earlier months)
  • August's bonus looks only at August's invoices; September's only at September's.
  • Unpaid money never expires. If a customer pays a September invoice in November, that part of September's bonus pays in November — even in the next cycle.
  • Voided and written-off invoices count for nothing.
ExampleEarned $99.40 in August, 42% of August's invoices paid → $41.72 released. September: $91.20 earned, 33% paid → $29.79. Payable now $71.51. As the rest of August gets paid, August keeps releasing.

6What you can do

  1. Open "Falling behind" — an early visit to a slipping customer beats everything.
  2. Help customers settle invoices on time — your collected % rises and "Payable now" follows.
  3. Place more lines, land new accounts — every line pays, and a new account pays extra.
  4. After your first sign-in, set your own password under My password.

7FAQ

Why is my collected % lower than a teammate's?

It's the customers, not you: big chains pay slowly, independents pay fast. Nothing is lost — it pays later.

Does a down month take money back?

No. What's been paid is never clawed back; a down month's growth is $0, and it pays again once growth climbs past what's already been paid on.

An account moved to someone else — do I lose out?

No. The account's history goes with it; it stops counting for your team and isn't charged against you either.

Why is growth split equally when some of us sell more?

Because accounts are hard to attribute to one person. Contribution and new-account bonuses are still yours alone.
