Teams — who earns growth together

Growth is earned by a team on the accounts it owns and split equally among its members. A rep belongs to exactly one auto team — that is the team the 80%-of-orders rule counts their orders for. A team with the fallback flag (e.g. "Everyone") catches the accounts no auto team owns outright, when its members together wrote 80%. Accounts can also be pinned to a single rep by name on the Assignment page — that rep earns the growth alone. The house group earns no growth.

Moving a person is dated. A roster change counts from the month you choose: the 80% rule credits each past order to the team the person was on when they wrote it, and each month's split uses that month's roster — so months before the move keep their numbers. Money already paid stays paid. The only effect on the past is what you'd expect: none.
