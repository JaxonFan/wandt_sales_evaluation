Pay ledger — every chapter, until it is collected and paid

A chapter is an earning period (the Aug–Sep launch chapter, then one cycle a year from October). A chapter ending does not close its money: an invoice written in September may only be paid by the customer in November, and when it is, that rep's September earnings become payable here. Nothing is stranded by a cycle rollover. Collectable = earned × the share of that chapter's own billing that has been collected; it rises as receivables come in. Owed = collectable − paid, floored at $0, so a recomputation can never take back money already released. Payments settle the oldest chapter first.
