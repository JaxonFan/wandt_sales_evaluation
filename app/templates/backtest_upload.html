What happens on import
  • Sales: rows from staff who have left are kept with no bonus credit, so inherited accounts have a real history and never show phantom growth. If the file has a Void Status column, voided invoices are excluded and recorded in one go.
  • Paid: matched to sales by invoice number. If a file fully covers a month and an invoice we held as paid is no longer in it, that payment was reversed — it drops out and its bonus claws back. A month the file doesn't cover is left alone.
  • After any import the dashboard, pay figures and account lists recompute on the next page load.
